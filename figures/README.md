# Figures

Drawn by `scripts/d6_figures.py` from `out/` (matplotlib, reference palette of the dataviz skill). Every figure comes in a light and a dark version, as PNG (200 dpi) and SVG. They are static, so there is no hover; the numbers are in the tables.

- `fig1_tp53_retrocopies` shows TP53 retrocopy loci for the 390 assemblies with a non-sparse retrocopy scan, sorted, with the median (1) and the two elephants (22 and 20) far above the next assembly (10). It does not show whether the copies are functional, and it leaves out the 98 sparse and 74 missing scans.
- `fig2_known_losses` shows, for UCP1, F12, KLKB1 and MMP20, the share of assemblies per clade that TOGA calls lost, with "lost / called" in each cell (cetaceans boxed: 37/37, 36/36, 32/32 and 6/33). It does not show individual species, and the uncertain (`UL`) and missing calls are left out of the denominator.
- `fig3_assembly_quality` plots the share of genes with 2+ ortholog loci against contig N50, with the 21.3% threshold (3 x median), the 25 assemblies with `qc_false_duplication` and the 10 more from the pilot's list of 35. It shows that N50 does not separate the bad assemblies; it does not prove which individual duplications are false.
- `fig4_lifespan_vs_mass` plots maximum lifespan against adult body mass (log-log) for the 357 AnAge species in the table, coloured by the residual from an ordinary least-squares line, with human from AnAge as a reference point. The line ignores phylogeny, so the residuals are descriptive only.
- `fig5_outliers` shows the top 5 retrocopy outliers (robust z) for 12 species, plus TP53 for the two elephants with its genome-wide rank (13 and 35). It shows that genome-wide TP53 is not the top elephant outlier. Panels marked with a dagger come from sparse scans and should not be compared with the others. It does not say whether any outlier is biologically real; the naked mole-rat TCF12 at the top of its panel is very likely not (see `out/nmr_check.log`).
